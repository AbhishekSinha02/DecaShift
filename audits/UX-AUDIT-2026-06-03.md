# Donnibo UX Audit — 2026-06-03

> **Two auditors. Two grades. One honest report.**
> Audited by simulating two real user types:
> - **Arya** — Grade 5, 11 years old, smart kid, uses a ₹8,000 Android phone
> - **Arjun** — Grade 11, 16 years old, board-exam-focused, uses his phone 4+ hrs/day

---

## Engagement Scores

| Dimension | Arya (Gr. 5, age 11) | Arjun (Gr. 11, age 16) |
|---|---|---|
| First impression (signup → home) | 2 / 5 | 3 / 5 |
| Quiz experience | 3.5 / 5 | 4 / 5 |
| Result screen | 3 / 5 | 3.5 / 5 |
| Home screen clarity | 2.5 / 5 | 3.5 / 5 |
| Gamification (XP, avatar, lucky Q) | 4 / 5 | 3 / 5 |
| Social (challenge, share) | 4.5 / 5 | 4 / 5 |
| Journey / progress screen | 1.5 / 5 | 2 / 5 |
| Would open tomorrow? | 3 / 5 | 3 / 5 |
| **Overall** | **3.0 / 5** | **3.2 / 5** |

**Verdict:** The app has the right bones — gamification, challenges, personalization — but loses both users at the same two failure points: the signup wall and the empty Journey screen. Fix those two and scores jump to 4+.

---

## Section 1 — Signup & Onboarding

### Arya's experience

> *"It's asking me for my email? I don't have one. My mom's email? And it needs my mobile number too? This is like a bank form."*

**What's wrong:**
- Mobile number is marked `*` required — Grade 2-6 students don't have phones. Parents' number creates hesitation.
- "Free forever. No credit card required." means nothing to a child who doesn't own a credit card.
- The form shows: Name, Email, Mobile, Password, Confirm Password, Grade, Regional Language — **6 fields before seeing the app**. Duolingo shows the app first, sign-up later.
- No "try without signing up" path. A kid who heard about this from a friend can't look inside before committing.
- The tagline "See yourself grow." is abstract. A kid wants to know "what will I do here?"

**Bug filed:** BUG-015 (placeholder WhatsApp support number), UX-001 (mobile required)

### Arjun's experience

> *"It's fine. Email + password, standard. But no Google sign-in? And there's no forgot password link on the sign-in screen. If I forget my password I'm screwed."*

**What's wrong:**
- No forgot-password flow (tracked separately as P2-T017).
- No social login (Google) which every competitor offers.
- Strong password not enforced — "Min 6 characters" only.

## Section 2 — Home Screen

### Arya's experience

> *"There's so much stuff. The flame thing, numbers, then a quest thing, then a card, then more cards. Where do I click?"*

**Visual hierarchy failure (mobile, 375px):**
```
[Header: Donnibo logo + name + avatar]         ← OK
[Subject tabs: Math | Science | English ...]   ← OK
[Streak bar: 🔥 0 days · —% accuracy · 0 sessions · 0m practiced · Best: 0d]  ← TOO DENSE
[Greeting: "Hello, Arya" + two lines of text]  ← FINE
[Daily Quest: Today's Quest 0/3 + chips]       ← GOOD but small chips
[Flash Drills: ⚡ Tables × Squares ² ...]      ← GREAT
[Today's Mission card]                         ← GOOD
[This Week · 2-8 Jun (N sets)]                ← GOOD
[Last Week · collapsed]                        ← GOOD (new)
[Topic rows · collapsed]                       ← GOOD (new)
```

**Issues for Arya:**
- Streak bar has 7 data points on one line. The only one she cares about is the flame number. Everything else is noise at this age.
- "Today's Quest 0/3" — she understands quest but not the number format. She'd expect it to show what to DO, not how many are done.
- The "Day 1 · Mon" meta label inside day-cards is confusing — she thinks of days by subject, not by Mon/Tue.
- No visual week map. Duolingo's fire-brick calendar is instantly understandable.

### Arjun's experience

> *"The accuracy badge on the Math tab (72%↑) — actually smart, I can see which subject I'm weak in at a glance. The weekly date range is clean too."*

**What Arjun likes:**
- Accuracy % badge on subject tabs — excellent at-a-glance performance signal.
- Week date range in row header — treats him like an adult.
- Flash drills hidden on non-Math tabs (just fixed) — correct behavior.
- Collapsible rows — "I don't need to scroll past 3 weeks of old stuff."

**What Arjun still wants:**
- "There's no 'I want to study Chapter 5 Thermodynamics' path. I can only go by week."
- The Today's Mission card shows only one subject (whatever tab is active). If he's on Physics but today's Math set is urgent, there's no cross-subject today-alert.

## Section 3 — Quiz Screen

### Arya's experience

> *"Ooh the lucky question star is SO COOL. I want every question to be lucky. Also wait what does the timer do? Does something happen when it goes up?"*

**What works:**
- Lucky Question `✨ Lucky Question · 2× XP` — best engagement moment in the app. Kids love it.
- A/B/C/D answer cards — large touch targets, clear labels.
- Challenge banner "First time here. No pressure" — reassuring.
- Confetti on correct lucky question — delightful.

**What doesn't:**
- Timer "00:00" with no explanation. Kids think it's a countdown. **Stress without purpose.**
- "Timer ON" toggle is a tiny ghost button in the top-right. Kids will accidentally tap it.
- No back/exit button. If Arya wants to stop mid-quiz (her mom's calling), she has no out. Browser back will break the session.
- "Next →" appears after submit but "Submit Answer" disappears. The swap is fast — sometimes kids tap Submit again and nothing happens (it's hidden), confusing.
- Wrong answer: explanation box appears but on a small phone, user has to scroll down to see it AND then scroll back up to press "Next →". Bad for flow.
- No hint. No lifeline. Complete guessing with no help option.

### Arjun's experience

> *"The explanation after a wrong answer is the most useful thing in this app. But I want to be able to come back and read all explanations after the session, not just one at a time."*

**What works:**
- Explanation quality (when present) is strong.
- Timer is useful for board exam time management practice.
- "Can you beat it today?" banner is competitive — Grade 11 students respond to challenge.
- Lucky question 2× XP — slightly childish but still fun.

**What doesn't:**
- **No difficulty label** on questions — "is this a board-level question or easier?"
- **No pause/resume** — studying in short bursts (between classes) means losing partial progress.
- If you get 0/10, the "Needs Work" badge on the result screen is demoralizing. Arjun has a board exam in 3 months. He needs coaching, not a judgment badge.

## Section 4 — Result Screen

### Arya's experience

> *"7 / 10 — I did okay! But what did I get wrong? I see the X marks in the table but clicking them does nothing. I want to see why I was wrong."*

**Critical miss:** The result table shows every question with ✓ or ✗ and time. But tapping a ✗ row does **nothing**. The explanation shown during the quiz is permanently gone after moving to the next question.

**What works:**
- The win message is personalized and kind ("Strong start." etc.) — great.
- "⚔ Challenge a Friend" — Arya WILL use this. This is the #1 viral moment.
- The score is big and clear.

**What doesn't:**
- "Session Complete" header — adult language. Could be "Done! 🎉" or "You finished!"
- "Needs Work" badge is harsh for a child. Consider "Rising Star ⭐" or "Keep Practicing 💪".
- The result table with # / Question / Result / Time — the question text truncated to 60 chars is often unreadable.
- "Back to Goals" — a Grade 5 kid doesn't think in "goals". Call it "Back to Home" or "Go Back".
- No "see what I got wrong" flow. The table is a list with no action.
- +XP display is small. If a kid leveled up, SHOW IT BIG.

### Arjun's experience

> *"The trend block (this week vs last week) is genuinely useful. But I want to tap on a ✗ question and re-read the explanation. And I want to see my accuracy per topic, not per set."*

**Critical miss same as Arya's:** No wrong-answer review.

**What Arjun specifically wants:**
- Topic-level breakdown — "7/10 overall, but 2/4 on Kinematics and 5/6 on Newton's Laws"
- "Challenge" button should also show me the challenge link text so I can copy it to share in a group chat (not just WhatsApp).

## Section 5 — Journey Screen

### Both Arya and Arjun:

> *"This is barely anything. Avatar, level, a number. Where's all my history? Where's my subject breakdown? This feels like a half-finished screen."*

**What's there:**
- Avatar with level ring ✓
- Level + XP bar ✓
- Streak count ✓
- Stats band (sessions / questions / accuracy) ✓

**What's missing (placeholders in HTML):**
```html
<div id="journey-mastery"></div>   <!-- empty -->
<div id="journey-badges"></div>    <!-- empty -->
<div id="journey-album"></div>     <!-- empty -->
<div id="journey-replay"></div>    <!-- empty -->
```

The four most interesting sections — mastery, badges, sticker album, journey replay — are all empty divs. A user who opens "My Journey" expecting growth tracking sees only a level number. **This is a trust-destroying moment.**

## Section 6 — Settings

### Both users found:

1. **Version shows "v3.9"** — the actual app is v4.3 per git commits. Looks like the version was never updated after the big rebuild. Minor but erodes trust if a user looks.

2. **WhatsApp support is `+91 9876543210`** — this is a placeholder dummy number. If ANY user tries to reach support, it goes nowhere. **This is a P0 bug if the app is in any user's hands.**

3. **"Your data is stored on your device"** — Arjun immediately said "so if I change phones I lose everything?" Cross-device sync is known-missing (P2-T046), but the settings copy makes it explicit and alarming.

4. **No "Kannada / Bengali / Gujarati"** in regional language options — covers only 6 languages, missing the 4 largest regional language bases after Hindi.
