# M-T005: The 30-Second School-Gate Demo Script

**Priority:** M1 | **Type:** Sales Enablement / Intern Script | **Status:** Ready to use

> The school gate at pickup time is the highest-intent, lowest-cost channel you have: parents
> standing idle for 10–15 minutes, every one of them with a school-age child, many already
> chatting to each other. This script turns that idle window into a free-trial signup in 30
> seconds — by *showing* the living-content + concept-first product, not pitching it.
>
> **Rule #1: Show, don't tell.** The phone does the selling. The intern narrates 6 lines.
> **Rule #2: The goal of the 30 seconds is ONE thing — get the QR scanned (free trial started).**
> Not to explain pricing. Not to close a sale. Just: phone in their hand → trial started.

---

## Before You Walk Up (phone pre-loaded — never demo live-loading)

1. App already **open** to a Grade 6 Math (or the local-board grade) **concept card** — the
   screen that *teaches* the concept before the question. Screen brightness up.
2. A **visual question** is the very next tap. Rehearse so it's one swipe away.
3. **QR sticker/card** in your other hand → `lucknowkids.in?ref=[your-code]`. Big QR, your
   name on it, "Free for 30 days. No card."
4. Phone charged, 4G on, app works offline anyway (so a dead signal never breaks the demo).

> If the app has to load, buffer, or log in during the demo, you've already lost them. The
> screen they see in second 1 must already be the impressive one.

---

## The Script (English) — 6 beats, ~30 seconds

**[0–5s] Approach + permission** (smile, hold phone facing them, not at them)
> "Sir/Ma'am — 10 seconds, your child's grade? … Grade 6? Perfect, watch this."

**[5–12s] The concept beat** (point at the concept card)
> "Before any question, the app *teaches* the concept — like a teacher, in 20 seconds.
> Your child learns first, then answers."

**[12–20s] The visual question beat** (swipe to the visual question, let them look)
> "Then a question — with a picture, not boring text. See? This is how kids actually enjoy it."

**[20–27s] The living-content beat** (the differentiator — say it slowly)
> "And new questions come **every week**. Other apps you pay once and it's frozen. This one
> grows with your child — all year."

**[27–30s] The ask** (turn the QR toward them — give them something to *do*, not decide)
> "Scan this — free for 30 days, no card, nothing to pay. Try it with your child tonight."

→ They scan. Done. **Do not talk price.** If they ask, → objection table below.

## The Script (Hinglish — how Lucknow actually talks)

**[0–5s]** *"Sir/Madam, das second — bachche ki class kaunsi hai? … Class 6? Bahut badhiya, ye dekhiye."*

**[5–12s]** *(concept card par ungli rakhein)*

## Objection Handling (1 line each — then back to "just try it free")

> **Never argue. Never over-explain.** Every objection ends the same way: *"Free hai, scan kar
> lijiye, raat ko try kijiye."* The trial does the convincing — your job is just to start it.

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Lead with the child's grade — it makes it personal in 3 seconds.
- Let them *hold* the phone for the visual question — touch = ownership.
- Say "every week" slowly. It's the line that beats every competitor.
- Get the **scan now**, even if they'll "look later" — the saved trial is the win.
- Hit parents standing in groups — one scans, the group follows.

**Don't**
- Don't explain pricing unless asked. Price is a conversion-time conversation, not a gate-time one.
- Don't demo on a loading screen. Pre-load everything.
- Don't pitch features. Show concept → visual → "every week." That's the whole story.
- Don't chase a hard "no." Move to the next parent — pickup time is a numbers game.

## Target & Tracking

- **Target:** ~15 parents approached per pickup window → ~5 scans → ~3 trials started.
- 2 school gates/day × 5 days = ~30 trials/week per intern from this channel alone.
- Every QR carries `?ref=[intern-code]` → signups attribute automatically (M-T002).
- Best gates: schools with Grade 5–8 strength, pickup 2:00–3:30 PM. Get the partner teacher's
  school first — warm ground.

## Why This Works (the psychology, for the intern to understand)

- **Idle + captive audience** — parents are standing with nothing to do.
- **Show beats tell** — they *see* their child learning in 8 seconds; no claim to disbelieve.
- **"Every week" is unbeatable** — no frozen-course competitor can say it.
- **Free + no card removes all risk** — the only decision is "scan or not," and scanning is free.
- **The real sale happens at home** — when the child uses it that night and the parent watches
  over the shoulder. The gate's only job is to start the trial. (See
  `POSITIONING-AND-GROWTH-ENGINE.md` — the concept card is the conversion trigger.)
