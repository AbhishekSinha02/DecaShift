# Donnibo — Mascot Design Brief

## Origin
Inspired by Donatello (TMNT) — the smartest turtle, bo staff wielder. Name: **Donnibo** (Donni + bo). Verb form: "Donniboed" = achieved/completed a task. "You Donniboed it!"

---

## Species: Mongoose

**Why mongoose:**
- Native to India — every Indian kid knows mongooses
- Famous as the smartest fighter (Rikki-Tikki-Tavi)
- Fights cobras = fights ignorance and hard problems
- Quick, agile, fearless — perfect personality match
- Zero other edtech apps use a mongoose. Owls are dead. Robots are dead.

---

## Style: Chibi

Big head, small body, large expressive eyes. Scales to app icon (32px) without losing character. Easy to animate. Kids immediately want to protect/root for chibi characters.

---

## Color Palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|------|-------|-----|
| Body fur | Deep violet-grey | `#4C3D6E` |
| Underbelly | Warm cream | `#F5E6C8` |
| Eyes | Electric gold | `#F59E0B` |
| Bo staff (base) | Dark wood | `#7C4A1E` |
| Bo staff (glow) | Electric cyan | `#22D3EE` |
| Aura/energy | Violet + cyan | gradient |

Purple-grey nods to Donatello without copying him. Gold eyes = wisdom. Cyan staff glow = electric intelligence.

---

## Bo Staff — The Signature Detail

The staff is not just a weapon — it is a learning tool. It glows brighter as the user levels up. Think of it as a knowledge meter expressed through the staff.

---

## The 6-Stage Evolution

Maps to the 6-stage avatar growth system in the app.

```
Stage 1 — SPARK
Tiny glowing orb. Two eyes visible inside.
No staff. Just potential.
"You just arrived."

Stage 2 — PUP
Small mongoose form. Fluffy, round.
Holds a plain twig (not even a staff yet).
Wide curious eyes. Slightly wobbly stance.

Stage 3 — ROOKIE
Full body visible. Confident posture.
Plain wooden bo staff, both hands.
Small bandana or wristband.

Stage 4 — FIGHTER
Leaner silhouette. Determined expression.
Staff has one glowing cyan tip.
Subtle speed lines on arms.

Stage 5 — CHAMPION
Battle stance. Both staff tips glowing.
Small electricity sparks around body.
Eyes sharper, more intense.

Stage 6 — DONNIBO
Full power form. Staff fully electrified.
Violet aura around entire body.
Calm confident smile — not angry, CERTAIN.
This is the "I know everything" face.
```

---

## Expression Sheet (7 states)

| State | Expression | When used |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| **Idle** | Slight smile, staff resting on shoulder | Default / home screen |
| **Thinking** | One eye squinted, staff tapping chin | Question loading |
| **Correct** | Both arms raised, staff overhead, big grin | Right answer |
| **Wrong** | Gentle frown, hand on chin, not angry | Wrong answer — encouraging |
| **Streak** | Running pose, staff trailing sparks | Active streak |
| **Level up** | Jumping, staff glowing full blast, eyes wide | Evolution / new stage |
| **Sleeping** | Curled up around staff, eyes closed, Zzzs | Not logged in 3+ days |

---

## What NOT to Do

- No turtle shell — zero TMNT visual connection
- No green skin
- Not angry or aggressive in default state — determined is different from angry
- Not holding a book — the staff IS the learning tool, no secondary "nerd" props
- Not wearing glasses — lazy "smart character" shortcut
