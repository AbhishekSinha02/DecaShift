# Donnibo — Marketing, Sales & Local Promotion Tasks

> **The model:** Local interns / marketing students promote a locally-developed product
> to local parents and teachers on a performance-based incentive. Zero fixed marketing cost.
> City-level rollout = bounded risk. Fail in one city → learn → replicate what works elsewhere.
>
> **The pitch:** Local guy. Local product. Local kids. Local partners. Local price.
> No national ad budget required. No VC needed. Just local trust, city by city.

## The Local Rep Model

```
Developer (you)
    │
    ├── City A: 2 marketing interns → parent groups + teachers + coaching centers
    ├── City B: 2 marketing interns → same playbook
    └── City C: 2 marketing interns → same playbook

Each city: punekids.in / nagpurkids.in / indorekids.in
Each rep:  their own ?ref=name tracking code
Each week: signups reported → incentive paid → playbook refined
```

**Incentive structure (suggested):**
- ₹5 per verified free signup (real email, completed first session)
- ₹50 per Pro conversion (when payment goes live)
- Bonus: ₹500 if 100+ signups in month 1

**Why this works:**
- Marketing student gets real sales experience + resume line
- You get city-anchored word-of-mouth with zero fixed cost
- Local rep has built-in credibility ("I'm from Pune, this is made for Pune students")
- City domain (punekids.in) reinforces the local story at every touchpoint
