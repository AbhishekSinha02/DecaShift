## Usage Notes for Reps

- **Always personalise** [Name], [City], [YOUR-CODE] before sending
- **Never mass-blast** the same message to multiple groups simultaneously — feels spammy, gets blocked
- **Send at the right time:** Parent groups: 7–9 AM or 8–10 PM. Teachers: 8–9 AM weekdays. Coaching centers: 11 AM–1 PM (between batches).
- **Follow up once** — 48 hours after first message. If no response after follow-up, move on.
- **Track every interaction** in your weekly report (M-T001 format)
