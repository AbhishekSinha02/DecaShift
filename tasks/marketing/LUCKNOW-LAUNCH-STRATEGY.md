# Lucknow Pilot — Single-City Launch Strategy

**Priority:** M1 | **Type:** Go-to-Market / Sales Leadership | **Status:** Active plan (2026-06-02)

> **Director's framing:** Launch in **one city — Lucknow — only.** Prove the full loop
> (acquire → engage → convert → retain) in a bounded, low-risk pilot. If the trial→paid %
> and the word-of-mouth hold here, the *same playbook* drops into the next city unchanged.
> If it breaks, the cost is one domain + a few weeks of intern time — and the failure is
> invisible to every other market.

Pricing for this launch lives in `P2-T013-subscription-tier-design.md`. This doc is the
**go-to-market** that wraps it.

---

## Why Lucknow First (founder's call — and it's defensible)

The city playbook (M-T004) ranked Lucknow #5 on coaching-market size. Launching it first is
the right call anyway, for reasons bigger than market size:

1. **UP Board is underserved.** BYJU'S / PW / Vedantu skew CBSE + national competitive exams.
   A **daily-practice habit aligned to UP Board** is a genuine wedge with little direct competition.
2. **Hindi-medium matters here** more than in Pune/Bangalore. Bilingual UI + Hindi-medium
   question support is a real conversion lever in Lucknow — and a moat the English-first
   incumbents won't bother to copy for a tier-2 city.
3. **Massive student density + exam-pressure culture** = high concentration of the exact buyer.
4. **A local connection beats the "best" city every time** (M-T004's own rule). If the founder
   has Lucknow ground contacts (a teacher, a coaching center, a parent circle), that warm entry
   is worth more than Pune's larger-but-cold market.

> Domain: **lucknowkids.in** (the local-story anchor — "local product, local kids, local price").

## The People (lean, local, performance-paid)

| Role | Count | Pay model | Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local partner / micro-influencer educator** | 1–2 | Paid promo (one-time ₹5–15k) **or** rev-share | One *trusted* Lucknow name — a known coaching teacher or a respected local tutor/school. Their endorsement = instant trust. NOT a generic Instagram influencer. |
| **Marketing intern** | 1–2 | ₹5/verified signup + ₹50/paid conversion + bonus | Parent WhatsApp groups, school-gate demos, coaching-center visits. MBA/BBA student who knows local parent circles. |
| **College intern (campus/social)** | 1–2 | Incentive + experience/certificate | Lucknow Univ / Integral / Amity / BBD / IIM-L. Campus posters, reels, sibling/younger-cousin referrals, content help. |

**Why 1–2 each, not more:** >2 per role creates coordination overhead and dilutes
accountability before you've proven the channel. Add headcount only *after* the pilot works.

**Local partner > influencer — be strict on this.** In a tier-2 city, one well-known coaching
teacher saying *"I recommend this for daily practice"* converts harder than 50k generic
reel views. Spend the paid-promo budget on **credibility, not reach.**

## The 6-Week Pilot Timeline

### Week 0 — Setup (before any spend)
- [ ] **Content cadence check (not a wall):** weekly drop is running and sits slightly ahead of
      the cohort you'll sell to. Velocity is proven (GTM in ~10 days) — the goal here is to keep
      the *living-content* story true, because "new every week" IS the pitch (see
      `POSITIONING-AND-GROWTH-ENGINE.md`). No need to pre-build a year; visible weekly building
      is the differentiator.
- [ ] Concept-first + visual-question revamp on the demo path — it's the trial→paid lever.
- [ ] Buy + wire `lucknowkids.in` (M-T004 Day-1 steps); verify city shows in hero.
- [ ] Razorpay Payment Links live for all three tiers + sibling add-on.
- [ ] Coupons created: `LUCKNOW500`, `STREAK21`, `WIN-[rep]`, `EXT45` (P2-T013).
- [ ] Hire 1–2 marketing interns + 1–2 college interns; assign ref codes (`lko-[name]`).
- [ ] Sign 1–2 local partner educators; give them `WIN-[partner]` + a Founding-Member angle.

### Week 1–2 — Seed the trial funnel (free, frictionless)
- [ ] **Local partner endorsement first** — their channel/class goes out before the cold push.
      A warm first wave seeds social proof for the intern push.
- [ ] Interns → first parent WhatsApp groups (Grade 5–8 focus) with EN+HI templates (M-T003).
- [ ] School-gate + coaching-center 5-min demos (highest-converting channel — `strategy_first_100_paid`).
- [ ] **Founding offer live:** "First 500 Lucknow families lock ₹1,499/yr for life + Founding badge."
      Urgency + pioneer pride from day one.
- **Founder's job:** be reachable on the landing-page WhatsApp number. One teacher call = ~35 students.

### Week 3–4 — Habit forms, consistency coupons kick in
- [ ] Trials now 2–3 weeks in — `STREAK21` auto-offers to the engaged (product-earned discount).
- [ ] Double down on whatever channel produced signups (parent groups / teacher / coaching).
- [ ] Sibling add-on push: parents with one engaged child → "add a sibling for ₹999/yr."

### Week 5–6 — Convert (the high-touch close)
- [ ] Trials hit the 30-day soft lock → upgrade screen (annual-led, child's real stats).
- [ ] Interns/partner personally follow up hesitant parents with `WIN-[rep]` (≤25% off) to close.
- [ ] `EXT45` for genuinely-interested-but-not-ready (buys two more weeks, not a lost lead).
- [ ] **Pay reps same-day** on every verified conversion (M-T004: never delay incentive).

## Success Metrics (what a director watches)

| Metric | Pilot target | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Free trials started | ~300 | Top-of-funnel reach proof |
| **Trial → paid %** | **18–22%** | **THE number** — tells you the premium price holds when human-closed |
| Sessions completed / signup | >60% | Real engagement vs vanity signups (M-T004 framework) |
| Paid users | ~60 in 6–8 wks | Proves unit economics in one city |
| Blended ARPU | ~₹1,700/yr | Validates the annual-led, sibling-add-on mix |
| Word-of-mouth ratio | track referrals | Founding badge + Concept Builder should make parents tell parents |

**Decision gate at Week 6:**
- Trial→paid ≥18% **and** session-completion >60% → ✅ replicate playbook to city #2.
- Acquisition fine but conversion <10% → ⚠️ price or content problem; fix before replicating.
- Signups <150 → 🔴 channel/rep problem; diagnose, don't scale.

## Director's Risk Register

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Premium price scares tier-2 parents | Free 30-day trial removes price at acquisition; price only at human-closed conversion; lead with ₹166/mo annual optic, never ₹399/mo. The living-content + concept-first story justifies the premium. |
| Content keeping pace with engaged cohort | Routine weekly drop kept slightly ahead of the cohort. Velocity proven (GTM in ~10 days). This is the *moat*, not a risk — see `POSITIONING-AND-GROWTH-ENGINE.md`. |
| Sales-led doesn't scale to 5K fast | Accept it — pilot proves *economics*, not scale; viral levers (Founding badge, sibling, share cards) run alongside |
| Coupon race-to-the-bottom by reps | Floor price ₹1,199/yr enforced in code; rep coupons capped at 25% |
| Local partner underdelivers | Rev-share option ties their pay to results; cap one-time spend until they prove a channel |
| Lucknow is a one-off, doesn't replicate | That's *why* it's a pilot — playbook is documented to drop into city #2 only after the gate passes |

## Relationship to the Old Strategy

This **supersedes the ₹79 viral-flood thesis for the launch motion**, but keeps its best
instincts: zero-friction acquisition, word-of-mouth as the growth engine, product quality as
the conversion multiplier, and city-by-city bounded risk. The difference: **we now charge a
premium and close with humans**, because a tier-2 sales-led motion supports it and proves real
revenue faster than a free flood. Revisit the pure-viral free model only if the pilot shows
the premium price suppresses word-of-mouth below replication threshold.
