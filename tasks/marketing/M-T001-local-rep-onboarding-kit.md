## Section 2 — Who to Approach (Your Targets)

### Target 1 — School WhatsApp Groups (Highest Priority)
Every school has parent WhatsApp groups per class. One message → 50–200 parents see it.

**How to get access:**
- Ask any parent you know personally to forward your message
- Connect with class representatives / PTAs
- Approach school office staff (they often manage groups)

**What you need from each group:** One parent to share the link. You don't need to be in the group yourself.

### Target 2 — School Teachers
One teacher who believes in the product = 30–35 students onboarded.

**Who to approach:** Math and Science teachers, Grade 5–9.
**Where to find them:** School gates (7:30–8:30 AM), staffroom during breaks.
**What to say:** "I have a free learning app with 6,000 practice questions. Can your students try it for a week? No cost, no commitment."

### Target 3 — Coaching Centers
Coaching center owners want to add value for their students. A free app recommendation costs them nothing.

**Who to approach:** Owner or manager, not just the receptionist.
**What to say:** "Can you recommend this to your students? We'll list [Coaching Center Name] as a Donnibo partner — your branding is visible to every student in [City] who uses the app."

### Target 4 — Stationery Shops + Bookstores Near Schools
They see parents and students daily. A physical flyer or counter card works here.

**Ask for:** Permission to place a small card or flyer at the counter.
**Offer:** "We'll list your shop as a Donnibo partner. Students who earn a 7-day streak get a discount coupon for your shop."

### Incentive Structure

| Action | Your Earnings |
|---|---|
| 1 verified free signup (real email + first session done) | ₹5 |
| 1 Pro subscription conversion (when payment is live) | ₹50 |
| 100 signups in Month 1 | ₹500 bonus |

## Section 7 — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if someone asks if it's safe for kids?**
A: "It's a browser-based app, no app download needed. No social features, no ads for free users, no personal data shared publicly. Completely safe."

**Q: What if they ask about Byju's or Khan Academy?**
A: "Donnibo has more features at a fraction of the price. Byju's is ₹2,000+/month. Khan Academy has no streak, no GK drills, no planner. Donnibo is ₹79/month and has all three."

**Q: What if they say they'll check it later?**
A: "No problem — I'll send you the link right now. Just try the free version today, no commitment needed."

**Q: What if a teacher wants a demo?**
A: "I can do a 5-minute demo on your phone right now. Or I can connect you with the developer directly on WhatsApp."
